@Graph Privacy Policy

Last updated: September, 2026

AtGraph LLC receives no data from @Graph. @Graph has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

AI Assistant (optional). If you use the AI Assistant, @Graph sends what the assistant needs for your request: your questions and commands, and the data, charts or images you ask it to work with. This may include values from your spreadsheets and pictures of your graphs. Nothing is sent until you choose a provider, agree to share with it, and ask a question.

It goes directly from your Mac to the provider you choose:

  • Apple Private Cloud Compute, on Macs that support Apple Intelligence; no account or key needed. Apple’s privacy terms apply.
  • Anthropic (Claude), OpenAI (ChatGPT), Google (Gemini) or xAI (Grok), using your own API key.

API keys. Your API keys are stored only on your Mac, in the macOS Keychain. AtGraph LLC never receives them.

How AI requests are used. @Graph does not use your AI requests or responses for any other purpose, including advertising, analytics or model training. Data sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider’s own privacy policy and data-retention practices, and your agreement with that provider.

Dictation. If you dictate a question, the speech is converted to text on your Mac by macOS. It is not sent anywhere.

Keynote. Sending a chart to Keynote happens on your Mac.

For support: support@atgraph.app
