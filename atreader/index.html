Overview

Received a figure from a colleague? An image from the lab next door? Or new data from your own lab? @Graph Reader by @Graph is the free, easy way to open and explore data in their original form (graphs, charts, images, or data tables) from a wide variety of sources, including @Graph and many other formats (see list of readable file types below).

Features

See it exactly as it was saved

Graphs, charts, and images from @Graph open in their original saved format, with the same colors, labels, scale bars, and layout.

Open a wide variety of data files

Data from Axon, HEKA, Igor Pro, Intan, Open Ephys, and more; Zeiss, Nikon, and Leica images, and ordinary photos; Excel, Numbers, and Prism tables. See every file type.

Zoom in on the details

Drag across a graph to zoom in, step through sweeps, and watch the Y axis fit what’s in view. Pinch, scroll, or drag to explore images.

Try a different view

Wonder how that bar chart would look as a box plot, a violin, or a scatter? Switch any chart’s type in one click.

Peek at the numbers

Open the spreadsheet behind any graph or chart, change a value, and watch the figure follow. Edits are never saved, so your files always stay exactly as they are.

Native for Mac, iPhone, and iPad

Built for Apple silicon Macs, iPhone, and iPad, with a modern experience on each in Dark, Light, or System appearance.

Files @Graph Reader Will Open

@Graph documents

  • Graphs, charts, images and spreadsheets saved by @Graph or @Stat.@graph

Tables and text

  • Apache Arrow and Feather.arrow .feather .arrows
  • Apache Parquet.parquet
  • Comma- and tab-separated text.csv .tsv
  • Excel.xlsx .xls
  • JSON and JSON Lines.json .jsonl .ndjson
  • Numbers.numbers
  • Plain text.txt .text
  • Rich text (its text).rtf

A workbook with several sheets asks which ones to open.

Statistics programs

  • GraphPad Prism.pzfx .prism
  • SAS transport.xpt
  • SPSS.sav .zsav
  • Stata.dta

A Prism project with several data tables asks which ones to open.

General science

  • HDF5.h5 .hdf5
  • Igor Pro.pxp .h5xp .ibw
  • LabVIEW TDMS.tdms
  • MATLAB.mat
  • NetCDF.nc .nc4 .cdf
  • NumPy arrays.npy .npz

A NetCDF file’s variables open as tables: those along one dimension together, each 2-D variable on its own.

Microscopy and imaging

  • Bitplane Imaris.ims
  • Bruker Prairie View T-series.xml
  • Leica.lif
  • NIfTI and Analyze.nii .hdr .img
  • Nikon.nd2
  • Olympus FluoView.oib .oif
  • OME-Zarr (its .zarr folder).zarr
  • PNG and JPEG.png .jpg .jpeg
  • TIFF and OME-TIFF.tif .tiff
  • Zeiss.czi .lsm

A folder of images opens as one image series, and a gzipped NIfTI file (.nii.gz) opens too. An Analyze .hdr/.img pair and an Olympus .oif (with its .oif.files folder) may ask for access to their folder.

Electrophysiology

  • AxoGraph.axgx .axgd
  • Axon (pCLAMP).abf .atf
  • Blackrock.ns1–.ns6 .nev
  • CED Signal (CFS).cfs
  • HEKA PatchMaster.dat
  • Intan.rhd .rhs
  • Neuralynx.ncs .csc .nse .nst .ntt
  • NWB (Neurodata Without Borders).nwb
  • Open Ephys.continuous .oebin
  • Plexon.plx
  • Spike2.smr
  • SpikeGLX (Neuropixels; its .bin or .meta).bin .meta
  • TDT.tsq
  • WinWCP and WinEDR (Strathclyde).wcp .edr

Recordings kept in several files (Open Ephys, TDT, Intan, Neuralynx, SpikeGLX and others) open from the file listed, and may ask for access to its folder.

Physiology

  • BIOPAC AcqKnowledge.acq
  • BrainVision (its .vhdr).vhdr
  • EDF and BDF.edf .bdf
  • LabChart (its MATLAB export).mat
  • PhysioNet WFDB (its .hea).hea

PhysioNet WFDB and BrainVision records open from their header, and may ask for access to its folder.

Genomics and cytometry

  • 10x Genomics and Matrix Market matrices.h5 .mtx
  • AnnData.h5ad
  • CLS class labels for a GCT file.cls
  • Flow cytometry (FCS).fcs .lmd
  • GCT expression matrix.gct
  • GEO series matrix.txt
  • Loom.loom

GCT, Loom, AnnData, 10x Genomics and GEO series matrix files open as heatmaps, and flow cytometry files as tables. A 10x matrix opens from its Cell Ranger .h5, its folder, or its matrix.mtx(.gz) with the features and barcodes beside it.

Chemistry and spectroscopy

  • JCAMP-DX spectra.jdx .dx .jcamp
  • mzML mass spectra.mzml

Spectra open as graphs, peak tables and centroid spectra as sticks; an mzML file’s chromatograms and spectra open as parts, its spectra grouped by MS level.

Astronomy

  • FITS (images, spectra and tables).fits .fit .fts .fz

Gzipped and tile-compressed FITS files (.fits.gz, .fits.fz) open too; images, spectra and tables each open in a window of their own.
